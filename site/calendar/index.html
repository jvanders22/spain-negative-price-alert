On a phone or Mac, "Apple / Outlook" opens your calendar app. "Google" opens Google Calendar's add-by-URL screen; on Android, subscribe on calendar.google.com from a browser, then it syncs. Anywhere else, copy the .ics link and add it as a calendar "from URL".

How fresh is it?

The feed itself updates within the hour after OMIE publishes. Your calendar app decides when to look: Apple Calendar lets you pick (set Auto-refresh to every hour or 5 minutes); Google Calendar refreshes subscribed calendars only every 12 to 24 hours and has no refresh button, so with Google an event can show up late, sometimes after the hours have passed. If you need it on time, use the RSS feed or the email alert instead.

What the events mean

  • Confirmed: the OMIE day-ahead result for tomorrow. These prices are final.
  • The Spain feeds include the PVPC (regulated tariff) price for those hours and the 2.0TD period. Grid tolls and charges still apply, so your bill is never zero; these are simply the cheapest hours.
  • If a window is withdrawn it stays in the calendar marked "Cancelled" for a few days.
  • Events are marked "free" (not busy), so they never block your schedule.
