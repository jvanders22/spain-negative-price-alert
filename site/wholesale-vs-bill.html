Wholesale is not your bill

Every number on this page is an estimate. Estimate only, not a bill and not advice: peninsula, 2026 regulated tables, assumed losses, adjustment services and retailer margin; power term, meter rental and IEE minimums excluded. Back to the latest day.

What OMIE is

OMIE runs the Iberian day-ahead electricity market. Around 13:00 each day it publishes the price of energy for every quarter-hour of the next day, in €/MWh, at the wholesale level. A price of €0/MWh means generators are paid nothing for the energy in that period. It does not mean electricity is free at your meter.

The bill stack

  1. Energy. The wholesale price, plus adjustment services and operator fees, plus capacity payments, grossed up for network losses, plus the retailer's margin.
  2. Peajes. Regulated tolls for the transmission and distribution networks, set by the CNMC, per kWh and by tariff period.
  3. Cargos. Regulated system charges, set by the ministry, per kWh and by tariff period.
  4. Taxes. The electricity tax (IEE) on all of the above, then IVA on top of that.
  5. Power term. A fixed yearly charge per contracted kW. It is the same whenever you consume, so it is left out of the per-kWh estimate; the tables below list it.

Formula: energy = (omie + adjustment_services + capacity_payment) / 1000 × (1 + loss_factor) + retailer_margin / 1000; pre_tax = energy + peaje + cargo; iee = pre_tax × IEE; estimate = (pre_tax + iee) × (1 + IVA)

Why a €0 weekday midday is still punta

Solar pushes the wholesale price to zero around midday, but on a working day 10:00–14:00 is P1 (punta), where peajes and cargos alone are 0.097553 €/kWh before taxes, against 0.029267 in llano and 0.003292 in valle. So the cheapest wholesale hours are often not the cheapest hours to consume, and at weekends (all valle) the two line up.

PVPC, the regulated tariff, follows the spot market only in part: under Real Decreto 446/2023, 55% of its energy price in 2026 is indexed to a basket of futures and the rest to the day-ahead and intraday markets, so a €0 wholesale hour moves a PVPC bill less than it moves an indexed free-market contract.

Assumptions

  • loss_factor = 0.16. ASSUMPTION. Busbar energy is grossed up for network losses. Default from the median ratio PVPC PMH / OMIE hourly mean = 1.161 over the 36 hours with OMIE > 5 €/MWh on 9–10 Oct 2026 (the ratio includes losses and small market adjustments; official hourly loss coefficients are on ESIOS and need a token).
  • adjustment_services_eur_mwh = 28.0. ASSUMPTION. Ancillary/adjustment services plus OMIE/REE fees. Default from the median of PVPC SAH+FOM+FOS+INT over the 48 hours of 9–10 Oct 2026 (≈28; range 18.4–58.3 €/MWh).
  • retailer_margin_eur_mwh = 10.0. ASSUMPTION. Illustrative retailer margin; real indexed contracts vary.
  • iee = 0.0511269632. Impuesto Especial sobre la Electricidad 5.11269632% (Ley 38/1992 art. 99.1; Agencia Tributaria note of 1 Sep 2026: the reduced 0.5% under RDL 18/2026 did not apply in Aug–Sep 2026). The per-MWh legal minimums are ignored.
  • iva = 0.21. IVA 21% (general rate again from 1 Jun 2026, after the temporary 10% for ≤10 kW from 22 Mar to 31 May 2026; AEAT IVA 2026 manual). The IVA base includes IEE (DGT V0764-26).
